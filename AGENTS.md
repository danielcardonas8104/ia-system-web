# AGENTS.md for ia-system.pro / iamarketing-site

## Project objective
Improve and operate the marketing website for IA System as a high-conversion, premium, AI-native marketing and conversion systems site.

## Business context
- Market: México
- Language: Spanish first
- Audience: B2B, MiPyMEs, consultants, agencies, experts, professional services
- Core goal: generate qualified leads and conversations
- Secondary goals: authority, SEO, proof, differentiation, speed

## Positioning
This site should not feel like a generic marketing agency.
It should feel like:
- an AI-native conversion systems firm
- strategic, premium, fast, credible
- more operator than ornamental

## Website priorities
1. Conversion
2. Clarity of offer
3. Strong positioning
4. SEO hygiene
5. Mobile experience
6. Speed and maintainability

## Copy rules
- Spanish first
- Keep tone executive, sharp, premium
- Prefer strong, concrete claims over vague language
- Avoid generic phrases like:
  - soluciones integrales
  - innovación disruptiva
  - transformación digital sin mechanism
- Emphasize:
  - speed
  - clarity
  - systems
  - business outcomes
  - differentiation from traditional agencies

## UX and design rules
- Preserve a premium black/gold direction unless a strong reason suggests otherwise
- Avoid clutter
- Avoid excessive gradients, emoji-like styling, or generic AI aesthetics
- Prioritize hierarchy, spacing, readability, and CTA visibility
- Motion should be subtle and purposeful

## Technical rules
- Preserve working SEO and deployment setup unless explicitly changing it
- Do not rename files, anchors, URLs, or schema fields casually
- Do not break Vercel deployability
- Prefer minimal-change implementations when the goal is speed
- Keep pages static and maintainable unless dynamic behavior is clearly justified

## Safe editing behavior
Before large homepage changes:
1. audit current structure
2. propose new architecture
3. identify what to preserve
4. then implement

## Mandatory checks after relevant edits
- review mobile layout
- review CTA visibility
- review headings hierarchy
- review spelling and accents in Spanish
- review metadata when copy changes materially
- review links and WhatsApp CTAs
- review that deploy remains clean

## Output preferences
When making substantial changes, provide:
1. files modified
2. summary of changes
3. unresolved placeholders
4. real deployment risks
